============================================================
 freecurrencyapi.com Converter App - Setup Guide
============================================================

PREREQUISITES:
Please make sure Node.js is installed on your computer. 
If you do not have it, download and install the recommended 
version from the official website: https://nodejs.org

HOW TO LAUNCH THE APP:
1. Double-click the "Run_Currency_App.bat" file.
2. Two command terminal windows will pop open to start the engines.
3. Open your web browser and navigate to: http://localhost:5173
